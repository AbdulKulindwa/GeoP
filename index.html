Core Features

🗺 Mapping Tools

Create accurate field maps with GPS and digital basemaps.

📐 Strike & Dip

Measure orientations directly with your phone sensors and compass.

📑 Logging Tools

Build stratigraphic logs and lithology records instantly in the field.

🌐 Georeferencing

Attach notes, samples, and photos to precise GPS locations.

🤖 AI-Powered Field Assistant

GeoOne smart AI ensures your measurements and logs are accurate, consistent, and complete. It is like having an expert geologist by your side.

✔ Error Checking

AI validates strike & dip against Right-Hand Rule and flags errors.

💡 Smart Suggestions

Prompts you to add missing details like grain size, structures, or lithology descriptors.

📊 Pattern Recognition

Groups related measurements (e.g., folds, faults) and suggests interpretations.

🗂 Organized Data

AI cleans and formats notes, logs, and maps for easy export and reporting.
